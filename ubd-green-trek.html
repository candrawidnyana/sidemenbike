GREEN TREK

Complete your holiday in Bali with our Green Trek tour to enjoy the beauty of mother nature. Our guide will take you deep into the rainforest, rice fields, local temples, and Balinese houses, while learning the famous Subak irrigation system. The tour will end at an organic field and lunch will be served with authentic Balinese delicacy.
